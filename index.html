Why Meal Loop?

A Smarter, Safer Loop for Every Meal

Over 30% of commercial kitchen food is discarded simply due to inaccurate demand planning and disconnected donation logistics. Meal Loop solves this at the root.

Our Core Promise: Don't Waste Food, Feed the Future

From corporate cafeterias and university hostels to large banquet halls, Meal Loop provides automated AI forecasts so you prepare only what is needed. When surplus inevitably arises, our system guarantees safe, immediate redistribution to local community kitchens before food spoils.

AI Demand Forecasting

Smart portion calculations analyzing historical 3-day averages, shift headcounts, and dynamic factors to stop over-preparation before cooking starts.

FSSAI Safety Standards

Comprehensive food temperature checkpoints, 4-hour shelf life verification, and automated compliance logs ensuring safe consumption.

Instant NGO Calling

Direct 1-click mobile calling and automatic driver dispatch to pre-vetted NGO shelters within your immediate kitchen radius.

CO2e Environmental Audit

Real-time quantification of carbon emissions avoided (+2.0 kg CO2e per meal) with downloadable official CSV audit documentation.
